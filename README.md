# E-Commerce Sales Insights Using SQL

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing an e-commerce sales dataset using SQL to uncover meaningful business insights. The analysis explores revenue performance, customer behavior, product trends, marketing effectiveness, and customer retention metrics.

The objective is to demonstrate practical SQL skills while answering real-world business questions that can support data-driven decision-making.

---

## Dataset Information

The dataset contains transactional e-commerce sales data, including:

* Order Details
* Customer Information
* Product Information
* Revenue Data
* Payment Methods
* Referral Sources
* Order Status

---

## Business Questions Answered

### 1. Data Overview

Reviewed the dataset structure and records to understand available information before analysis.

### 2. Total Revenue

Calculated the total revenue generated from all orders.

### 3. Product-Wise Sales Analysis

Identified the top-performing products based on revenue and quantity sold.

### 4. Referral Source Performance

Analyzed which referral sources generated the most customers and revenue.

### 5. Order Status Distribution

Examined the distribution of shipped, returned, and cancelled orders.

### 6. Payment Method Analysis

Analyzed customer payment preferences and revenue contribution by payment method.

### 7. Most Returned Product

Identified the product with the highest number of returns.

### 8. Average Order Value (AOV)

Calculated the average revenue generated per order.

### 9. Monthly Revenue Trend

Analyzed monthly revenue patterns to identify seasonal trends.

### 10. Revenue at Risk

Calculated potential revenue loss caused by cancelled and returned orders.

### 11. Repeat Customer Rate

Measured the percentage of customers who made multiple purchases.

### 12. Top Customers by Revenue

Identified customers contributing the highest revenue.
